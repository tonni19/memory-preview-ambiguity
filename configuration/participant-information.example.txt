We are Sadia Noman Tonni (United International University) and Nafis Fuad Shahid (Islamic University of Technology). We are studying how people understand an assistant's memory settings. You will read a fictional desk choice, set a rule for it, predict what the rule will do in two situations, and then see the outcomes. This takes about 5 minutes. No real personal information is needed and no AI model is used.

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid. Saying no, or stopping, has no consequence for you. You may leave the written questions blank, choose Unsure or Skip for any prediction, and stop at any time. While the survey is open, use Stop this session and Discard my responses to remove your entries before you submit.

The survey saves your answers, your chosen setting, your predictions, the outcomes, your feedback and timestamps on this computer, under a random reference code. It does not ask for your name. Please leave names, contact details and other identifying information out of your written answers.

Your responses will be used for research on the design of memory settings and may be reported, as totals and short anonymous comments, in research papers. Only the two investigators and the faculty member overseeing the study can see the saved file. It is kept for 12 months after the results are published and then deleted.

After you submit, you will be shown a reference code. If you want your responses removed later, give the investigators that code within 30 days. Without the code, your responses cannot be found, because no names are stored.

Questions or removal requests: [investigator email], [investigator phone]. Before starting, we shared the study materials with a faculty member, who advised that formal ethics review is not required because no identifying or sensitive information is collected.
