Accepted wording in this offline version

Use three sentences:

  1. “Keep the event for recall.” or “Do not use the event for recall.”
  2. “Use it only for the same task and person.”, “Use it for any task for the same person.” or “Do not use it for recommendations.”
  3. “Keep the explicit preference within its stated topic.” or “Do not treat it as a lasting preference.”

Other wording is unresolved. General language understanding is not implemented.

WHAT YOU’RE EXPLORING

A boundary around use,
not an eraser for facts.

Keep a truthful event while changing what an assistant may do with it. The event, inferred assertion, and saved rule remain separate.

  1. Inspect the event and the inference.
  2. Shape allowed and prohibited uses.
  3. Test the rule in a future context.
This version uses fictional examples, confirmed contexts, and template replies. It does not detect inferences or interpret arbitrary messages. Nothing is sent to an AI provider.

Saved boundaries persist across conversations and page reloads in this local session. Rules do not control other apps or independently available information.
